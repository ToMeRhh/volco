Volco OLED screens

Simulated SSD1306 screens, drawn at the real 128×64 resolution and then enlarged. The fonts are close to what the firmware would use, not exact.

Panel colour

Many 0.96″ modules have two colours: the top 16 pixel rows are yellow and the rest blue, fixed in the glass. If yours does, the layout should use that band on purpose. Layout B does.

Question: what does a normal button's screen show?

The same four buttons, drawn in each layout. Text that doesn't fit holds for 2 seconds, scrolls to its end, then jumps back.

Layout C, nudged to fit a yellow band

A 32-pixel icon and a name can't both fit below row 16, so the nudged version moves the state line up into rows 0–15 and uses a 28-pixel icon (rows 18–45). On your white panels the band is invisible. The left column shows it in white, the right in yellow + blue.
